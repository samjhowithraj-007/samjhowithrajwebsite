The agent loop: think, act, check

Agentic AI with Bunty, lesson 4 of 6. simple English notes, free.

Open the interactive lessonWatch on YouTube

The idea

Raj said: "Bunty, find the cheapest flight to Delhi for tomorrow." Bunty failed the first time, and still finished the job. How?

Bunty works in a loop. Think: "I need to open a flight site." Act: search the site. Check: oops, this flight is for the day after tomorrow!

Bunty fixes the date and goes round again: think, act, check. Until the goal is done.

  • Try 1: search, check, wrong date. "Sir, the date was wrong. Trying again."
  • Try 2: search, check, correct: "Sir, tomorrow's 6 am flight. The cheapest!"

Try it yourself

  • Pick a task you do (booking a ticket, paying a bill). Write its think, act and check steps.
  • Write one thing the check step should catch, and what the agent should do then.

Remember

A chatbot answers once and stops. An agent stops only when the work is done. Next: Part 5, Planning.
