range(): 10 cups at once

Python with Chai, lesson 15 of 20. A short Hinglish video, simple English notes and code you can run, free.

Open the interactive lesson with Run button

The idea

range(1, 11) is a counting machine: it gives 1, 2, 3 … 10. Put it in a for loop and 2 lines do the work of 10.

The stop number is never included: range(5) gives 0 to 4, and range(1, 6) gives 1 to 5.

A third number is the step: range(2, 11, 2) jumps by 2 and gives 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.

The code

for i in range(1, 11):
    print(f"Cup {i} ready")

print(list(range(5)))
print(list(range(2, 11, 2)))

Output:

Cup 1 ready
Cup 2 ready
Cup 3 ready
Cup 4 ready
Cup 5 ready
Cup 6 ready
Cup 7 ready
Cup 8 ready
Cup 9 ready
Cup 10 ready
[0, 1, 2, 3, 4]
[2, 4, 6, 8, 10]

Try it yourself

  • Print the 5 times table: 5, 10, 15 … 50 (hint: a step of 5).
  • Count down from 10 to 1 with range(10, 0, -1).

Remember

range(start, stop, step): the stop never comes.
