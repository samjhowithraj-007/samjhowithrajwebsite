Goal: tell Bunty what "done" looks like

Agentic AI with Bunty, lesson 2 of 6. simple English notes, free.

Open the interactive lesson

The idea

Raj told Bunty "do something nice"… and Bunty switched off all the office lights!

The mistake was not Bunty's. Raj gave a question, not a goal.

An agent needs a clear goal: what to do, by when, and what "done" looks like. Then it knows when to stop.

  • Vague: "Increase sales."
  • Clear: "Email this week's 20 new leads, and give me the list of replies by Friday."

Try it yourself

  • Take one vague task from your work ("improve the report") and rewrite it as a goal: what, by when, and what done looks like.
  • Ask: how would an agent know it has finished?

Remember

A clear goal gives clear work. (Goal saaf, toh kaam saaf.)
