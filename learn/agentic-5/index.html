Planning: big goal, small steps

Agentic AI with Bunty, lesson 5 of 6. simple English notes, free.

Open the interactive lessonWatch on YouTube

The idea

Raj told Bunty: "Plan Mummy's birthday party for Sunday." Bunty panicked: "Sir, such a big job can't be done in one go!"

So Bunty made a plan. He broke the big goal into small steps, and did them one by one.

On every step he ran the loop from Part 4: think, act, check. Then one step failed, and Bunty changed the plan.

  • Step 1: order the cake. Check: ordered? Yes.
  • Step 2: message the guests. Check: sent? Yes.
  • Step 3: decoration. Check: the decorator is not coming! Bunty changes the plan and orders balloons online.

Try it yourself

  • Take a big job (a school trip, a family dinner). Break it into 3 small steps.
  • For each step, write how you will check it is done.
  • Pick one step that could fail. What is your Plan B?

Remember

A good agent breaks a big goal into steps, and re-plans when it needs to. Next: Part 6, Memory.
