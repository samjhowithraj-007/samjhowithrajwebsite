Chatbot vs agent

Agentic AI with Bunty, lesson 1 of 6. A short Hinglish video, simple English notes, free.

Open the interactive lessonWatch on YouTube

The idea

Ask ChatGPT to "book my Goa trip" and it writes a long answer. No ticket or hotel actually gets booked.

A chatbot (Bunty version 1) only gives advice: "Sir, you could write the email like this…". You still do the work yourself.

An agent (Bunty version 2) has a laptop, email and a calendar. It finds a free slot, sends the email and tells you the meeting is fixed.

An agent has three extra things:

  • Goal: you give it a task, not a question.
  • Tools: email, browser, calendar, so it can act in the world.
  • Loop: act, check the result, decide the next step, until the job is done.

Try it yourself

  • Write down 3 of your daily tasks. For each one, what would a chatbot do, and what would an agent do?
  • Which tools would the agent need?

Remember

A chatbot tells you; an agent does it.
