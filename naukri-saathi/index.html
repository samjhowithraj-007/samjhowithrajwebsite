Questions

Will this get me a job?

No. It helps you prepare better applications. Nobody can promise a job or a shortlist.

Is my resume stored?

In the demo it never leaves your browser. In AI mode it goes to the AI provider only to write your answer and we do not store it. See the privacy section.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. It does not auto-apply or sit in your interview. You send your own applications.

Are the salary numbers real?

They are estimates with a source and date, not promises. Until sourced data is added they are marked illustrative.

What if I am missing skills?
