Botu Chess

Beep boop! Botu loves chess. Play against Botu, or with a friend on the same phone.

How to play: tap a piece, then tap a dot to move. Dots show every legal move. Castling, en passant and promotion all work. Checkmate the other king to win! A game is a draw on stalemate, the 50-move rule, the same position 3 times, or only kings left.
