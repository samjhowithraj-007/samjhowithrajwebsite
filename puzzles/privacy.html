Privacy

The short version

  • Your child's progress (stars, best times, streak, sound setting, currency and cart) is saved only on your own device. We cannot see it.
  • No accounts. No sign-up. No ads. No tracking or analytics cookies.
  • We do not collect personal data from children.

Payments

Payments are taken by Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details go straight to Razorpay. We never see or store them. A parent or guardian must make every purchase; the "grown-ups only" question before payment is a simple check.

Shipping orders

For printed books, comics or stationery we need a delivery name, address and phone number. We use them only to ship and support that order, and keep them only as long as the law or the order needs.

Emails and WhatsApp

If you write to us or join our WhatsApp channel, we use your message only to reply. You can leave the channel at any time.

Third parties

  • Razorpay (payments). See its own privacy policy.
  • YouTube and WhatsApp links open those services, which have their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any order details we hold. Write to our contact email.
