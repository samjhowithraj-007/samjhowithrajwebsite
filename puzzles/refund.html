Refunds and cancellation

Club plans

  • Cancel any time from the Club page or by writing to us. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or by accident right after a renewal and you did not use the Club, write to us within 7 days and we will refund the charge.

Digital products (puzzle packs, PDF books, courses)

  • If the file does not arrive, does not open, or is not what we described, write to us within 7 days. We will fix it or refund you.
  • Because digital items cannot be returned, we do not refund when you simply change your mind after downloading.

Physical products (books, comics, stationery)

  • If an item arrives damaged, wrong or missing, write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you.
  • You can cancel an order before it ships for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it. For international payments this can take up to 10 working days.

How to ask

Write to our contact email with your name, the email or phone used for the order, and what went wrong. We reply within 2 working days.
