Shipping and delivery

Digital items

Puzzle packs, PDF books and Club access are available straight away in the app, or by an email or download link sent right after payment. If it has not arrived in 1 hour, check spam, then write to us.

Physical items (India only for now)

  • We ship printed books, comics and stationery only inside India. Orders from other countries cannot be shipped yet and are shown as "India only" or "Coming abroad".
  • Orders ship within 3 to 5 working days. Delivery usually takes 4 to 8 working days after shipping, depending on your pin code.
  • Shipping charge: shown at checkout, and the full total is shown before you pay.
  • We send a tracking link by email or WhatsApp when your order ships.

Wrong address or lost parcel

Check your address before paying. If a parcel is lost, write to us and we will send a new one or refund you, see the refund page.
